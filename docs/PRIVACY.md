# Privacy policy for the app “Klar TV”

[Deutsch](DATENSCHUTZ.md) · Last updated: 22 November 2026 · Developer: phillhf

Klar TV has no server of its own, no accounts, no ads and no usage analytics.

**Stored on the device:** your provider's credentials, favorites, history, playback positions, TV guide, downloads
and recordings. This data is kept in the app's private storage, is excluded from cloud backup and is removed when
you uninstall the app.

**Connections:**

- to your **IPTV provider** and the addresses it supplies (streams, images, TV guide). Many providers use
  unencrypted http; in that case your credentials are not encrypted on the way there either.
- to **TMDB** and **OpenSubtitles** only if you enter your own key. The title of the movie or series is sent;
  for OpenSubtitles only when you tap search.
- to **YouTube** only when you tap “Trailer”.
- **Chromecast:** when you open “Cast”, the Google Cast SDK looks for devices on your Wi-Fi. The SDK is made by
  Google and may send technical data of its own to Google. The selected device receives the stream address, which
  contains your credentials for Xtream providers.
- **External player:** the app you choose also receives the stream address.

**Permissions:** notifications (downloads and recordings), exact alarms (scheduled recordings), start after reboot
(to set scheduled recordings again), local network (servers on your own network). The app only accesses files in
folders you select yourself.

**Deletion:** You can remove a provider with its favorites and history in the settings. Uninstalling the app deletes
all of its data. The developer holds no data about you.

**Questions:** via [GitHub issues](https://github.com/phillhf/klartv/issues). Please do not post credentials there,
the page is public.
